# Answered Questions

**Question:** Can we get external data?  
**Answer:** No.

**Question:** Can we use outside tools for exploratory data analysis (EDA)?  
**Answer:** Yes.

**Question:** How to handle new teams?  
**Answer:** Despite a team being new, some of its players may already exist in the dataset and have historical data. We can use this history to derive insights about the new team.

**Question:** What happened in 2004?  
**Answer:** The 2004–05 NHL season would have been the National Hockey League's 88th season. The entire 1,230-game schedule, originally set to begin in October 2004, was officially canceled on February 16, 2005, due to an unresolved lockout that began on September 16, 2004.
